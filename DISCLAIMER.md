### 1. Nature of the Project

This repository (the "Project") is a **cybersecurity and protocol analysis
research** effort targeting the web client of the **Zhihuishu / Zhidao**
online education platform. It falls within the scope of **technical study
and academic discussion**.

The research aims to understand the engineering implementation of modern
web applications in the following areas:

- Unified identity authentication (CAS / SSO) and session lifecycle management
- Client-side transport encryption and key management
- Client behavior telemetry and server-side risk control
- Front-end code obfuscation and deobfuscation techniques
